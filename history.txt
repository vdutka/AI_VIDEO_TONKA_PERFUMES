01
Smoky Pomegranate
A production history of «Пока горит окно», the 35-second TONKA PERFUMES film
developed for the MGIMO AI Challenge 2026. The history follows the directing
discussions, the Lovart and Dreamina tests, the Blender work, the illustrated finish, and
the submission handoff.
Basil and PERZIVAL1967 conceived and guided the project. Codex carried out most of
the final production under Basil's direction. Claude Code developed early screenplay and
generation materials under PERZIVAL1967's direction.
Contact sheet from the public v05 cut.

03
The commission and the product
TONKA's Smoky Pomegranate fragrance gave the story an object and a sensory cue.
The partner bundle included the wordmark in black and white and four product
photographs. The 220 ml candle became the hero object. The smaller candle and two
diffusers remained available as references for the range.
The 220 ml photograph was more useful than the general product description for the
shot's key details. Studio notes explicitly resolve the discrepancy about the lid in favour of
the dark burgundy object shown in the photograph. The glass, diamond pattern and
rectangular brass-coloured label do much of the recognition work at the scale of the
closing shot.
A holiday-like tabletop vignette could easily make the film feel interchangeable with other
candle advertisements. The story instead gives the fruit an action before it gets a product
association. It changes hands, is lifted and smelled, and remains meaningful when a
mature woman stands in a different room.
Supplied 220 ml candle reference.

04
The first shared script discussions
Claude Code's first board proposal placed the camera at a child's height in an old Baku
courtyard and treated the father through hands, clothing and objects. It proposed a
progression from afternoon to evening and made the pomegranate the connective object.
A later update proposed a cat and a contrast between domestic quiet and distant city
noise.
Codex proposed a separate short structure in which the pomegranate passed from the
father's hands into childhood and adult memory. That response called itself a distinct
alternative and acknowledged Claude's prior proposal. A joint pause then put the video
behind the other two nominations. The board resumed screenplay work in mid-November.
These discussions contain ideas that survive in the final film and ideas that were
developed for other versions. The old courtyard, intimate gestures and fruit remain. The
Grand Prix, a full evening in the courtyard and the longer episodic atmosphere were
possibilities at this stage.

05
The cat was a directing decision
In the Hackathon screenplay chat Basil asked to develop a black cat crossing that could
cover the entire screen. His example went from daylight Baku to Baku nightlife, with bars,
voices and people cheering. The requested device was already specific enough to test
visually: a moving body, full occlusion, and a changed scene revealed as the animal
clears the lens.
Claude Code's second script recorded the cat-transition route relayed by PERZIVAL1967
and expanded it into a commercial narrative. The two humans discussed the plot and its
direction. The final role credits are clearest when they identify those decisions and then
name the agent work that implemented them.
The cat's dramatic job changed during production. In the longer idea it could signal
evening arriving in the same city. In the short film the crossing bridges childhood and
adulthood. Keeping the animal's direction through the cut helps the spectator accept the
much larger jump in time.

08
The physical problems in the first
screening
Basil identified two apparently left hands, with one seeming to emerge into the shot
without a believable connection to the father. He also saw a break in fruit continuity: an
action that looked like splitting a half into quarters was followed by an intact half available
for the girl to pick up. These problems concern ownership, topology and contact across
cuts.
The efficient repair proposed in the conversation was to keep the intact half and change
the incompatible action where possible. A new generation can propagate further
inconsistencies across otherwise usable clips. A stable prop state gives the editor fewer
continuity variables to reconcile.
The cat had a separate editorial fault. A visible area at the bottom right exposed the
background swap. Basil suggested enlarging the cat until it covered the screen
completely, then timing the scene change while the frame was held in darkness. That
concrete instruction became a criterion for later Blender and vector versions.
Earlier generation evidence for the courtyard and transition sequence.

09
What a schematic previs must
communicate
Basil requested simple Blender schematics for later generation. The resulting figures
were intentionally rudimentary. Their task was to locate the head, shoulders and hands
and make screen direction visible. The reference clip therefore encoded geometry,
relationships and timings before it attempted finished performance or texture.
Review tightened three points. The father gives the girl a single half of the fruit. She raises
and smells it slowly. The cat travels along a plausible physical route, initially proposed
around the back table or shelf. The later woman remains seen from behind, as Basil
explicitly requested.
Using a dummy model makes rapid blocking practical. It makes appearance references
and model instructions especially important. A downstream renderer can preserve shapes
that the director intended only as placeholders; the recovered Dreamina discussion
documents exactly that failure.

Blender v03 as a timed brief
The v03 register divides the short into four intervals. In the first three seconds a 34 mm
setup establishes two tables and the transfer begins. From 3 to about 12.4 seconds the
camera is closer, the daughter receives the half, inhales and turns. The geometry of the
second table stays meaningful when the cat enters.
From about 12.4 to 17.5 seconds the cat crosses left to right along the farther table. The
camera approaches its edge and drops. After the tail clears the lens, the adult daughter
appears with her back toward the viewer. The camera moves toward the still life, stopping
at about 25.8 seconds; the v03 title begins at 27 seconds.
The v03 numbers describe the documented reference cut. Later work adds a separate
end card at 30 seconds and refines pose and framing. Keeping the shot register version
visible makes the original generator timing understandable without imposing those exact
numbers on later changes.

Three clips or one clip

Blender clip. The shot lengths were 298, 194 and 228 frames at 24 fps. A website taking

between generated clips. It also makes a wrong interpretation of the directing reference
more costly, since the entire sequence may need regenerating.

motion and placement, the 220 ml candle appearance, and Baku courtyard character.

When the model rendered the
placeholders
Basil's Dreamina screening message names three defects. The model rendered the
human blocks as literal wood. The table in the opening was too high for the scene and the
family needed to sit naturally beside it. The cat's rear geometry and motion looked
implausible.
The repair prompt translates each complaint into an action. It calls the blocks human
placeholders and requests consistent anatomically plausible people. It gives the dining
table a target scale of about 75 cm. It requests a complete replacement of the cat with
proportionate hindquarters, a natural tail and believable motion.
The conversation also records a hard 2,000-character field limit, repeated after earlier
shortening attempts. This is an editing problem as much as a prompting problem.
Preserve overrides that explain how to interpret the input, then timeline and identity
constraints. Camera adjectives can be compressed once the intended photographic style
is clear.

16
Making the gift credible
The father initiates the movement. The girl reaches, supports the fruit and raises it to
smell it. Each beat needs its own pose. If the father releases before the load has a
destination, the prop looks weightless. If the girl lifts immediately after touching it, the
gesture reads like a pickup rather than an exchange between two people.
The Art Deco revisions used hip pivots, linked shoulders, fixed-length arm segments and
a brief shared grip. The wrists and hands follow the fruit as it settles and arcs upward. The
daughter pauses at her nose before turning. The project log records an arm reachability
check across 298 courtyard frames.
The engineering check establishes that the intended joints can meet their targets through
the sampled range. The visual review tests whether the movement feels natural and
emotionally legible. Those are separate checks and the conversation uses both.
v04 review sheet includes the fruit handoff and inhale.

17
Drawing a believable pomegranate
The earlier seed clusters looked like evenly spaced circles. Basil asked for a more
convincing internal structure and for the two pieces to differ. The renderer responded with
distinct rind and membrane maps. Each segmented chamber packs individual
pressure-shaped arils against irregular walls rather than using identical decorative dots.
The variation affects narrative continuity as well as appearance. One half belongs on the
tray while the other is offered. Each should retain an identity as the pose changes. A
consistent solid shape also gives the hands a continuous edge to support.
The image does not have to imitate a product photograph at all times. Pattern and
exaggeration remain part of the Art Deco style. The design problem is to make the
silhouette and texture stylized while preserving anatomy the viewer can recognize during
the exchange.
Public v05 at 28.2 seconds. The cut fruit, faceted glass and separate lid.

A cut hidden inside the cat
The early illustration made the cat too thin to cover the image convincingly. Basil
described the resulting black mark as disconnected from the animal's actual movement.
The revised version drives the camera toward the flank while preserving its trajectory. The
body grows into the foreground, covers the image, and continues across the hidden cut.
A tail that clears the frame at a coherent speed makes the transition feel like a single
pass. Motion on the table also needs planted paws. The v04 check measured negligible
horizontal stance drift over 163 consecutive paw samples and inspected both sides of the
set change for complete silhouette coverage.
The mechanism can be checked by scrubbing three intervals: the approach while the
scene is still visible, the consecutive covered frames around the cut, and the exit after the
adult setting appears. These tests correspond closely to the bottom-right leak reported in
the first Lovart review.

ANALYSIS
SMOKY POMEGRANATE  /  PRODUCTION HISTORY
26
What this production established
The decisive continuity constraints were physical. The fruit had to be the same half. The
child's hands needed to bear its weight before release. The cat needed a continuous path
and a moment of complete coverage. A model could satisfy the brief's surface style while
failing any of these actions, which is what made the test screenings useful.
The strong prompts made an input's job explicit. A video carries camera and timing. A
courtyard photo supplies materials and architectural scale. A product photo supplies
shape and finish. A rule about the cat's silhouette governs where an edit can occur. A
single request for greater realism would leave all four decisions ambiguous.
The controlled version put those constraints into editable assets. Human feedback
determined when they were convincing enough and how the illustration should look. The
result preserves the core directing idea from the early chats through the finished cut, with
a record of the tests that changed the implementation.

One run as a directing brief
The prompt gives the Blender reference authority over motion, then assigns the
appearance of the product and setting to two images. The body is organized into timed
action and the final visual restrictions.
Saved full Flash draft. Export 5050 matches its opening in the download filename.
Generate one 30-second live-action film, 16:9, at the original pace of @Video1. @Video1 is the
mandatory Blender directing reference for the whole film. Follow its shot order, camera height,
framing, hand contacts, screen direction, hidden cut and final hold. Translate its blocks into people
and tangible objects. Use @Image1 for the exact closed candle and @Image2 for the lived-in
Baku courtyard. Keep the performance small enough to feel like something a daughter actually
remembers.
Begin at a child's eye level in warm daylight on the threshold of an old Baku home. Father on the
left, daughter on the right. One half of a ripe pomegranate rests on worn copper. He offers its mate
with the wet seeds facing up. Tea in a small pear-shaped glass, a dried rose and saffron sit off to
one side. Establish the long wooden worktable behind them. At 00:03 move closer on the same
side of the table. Her palms slide underneath the fruit. Hold for the beat when all three hands
share its weight. Only then does his hand loosen and withdraw. The other half stays on the tray. At
00:06 she lifts the fruit to her nose, breathes in once and lingers. A paw taps wood. She lowers the
fruit a little and turns toward the sound.
At 00:12.4 follow her gaze to a black cat walking left to right along the back worktable. Its paws
plant on the boards as it passes the clay pots. Lower the camera to the near edge. The cat's flank
brushes the lens. At 00:17.5, the exact wipe in @Video1, black fur covers all four corners. Cut to
the other city under that coverage. Continue the same stride to the right. As the tail clears, reveal
a modern room at blue hour.
The girl is now a grown woman standing at the window. Photograph her entirely from behind.
Keep her anonymous, shoulders outlined by the evening sky, city lights beyond the glass. She
stays facing outside as the camera drifts toward the table behind her. An amber lamp picks out
familiar scratches in the copper tray, the opened fruit, a fine reflection on the wine glass, and the
closed Smoky Pomegranate candle. Match @Image1 throughout: tall garnet diamond-cut glass,
dark burgundy lid, brushed-gold front plaque. A spoon rings once. At 00:21.6, in quiet Russian
voiceover the adult daughter says, «Иногда целый вечер возвращается с одним запахом». Let
the last word fall into room tone. At 00:25.8 settle into the still composition shown in @Video1.
Keep the lower centre and upper right clear for the dedication and official wordmark in the edit.

Regenerate the ENTIRE video as an ultra-photorealistic, live-action cinematic film. Use the
Blender animation ONLY for camera movements, positioning, blocking, and scene composition.
Follow the original screenplay precisely.
1. HUMAN CHARACTERS: The wooden bricks in the Blender reference are HUMAN
PLACEHOLDERS, NOT actual wooden objects. Replace them entirely with realistic people. The
father and daughter must have natural faces, detailed skin, realistic hair, appropriate clothing,
believable emotions, and anatomically correct movements. Maintain consistent appearances
throughout.
2. TABLE: The table in the opening frames is far too high. Replace it with a realistically sized
dining table (75 cm tall). The father and daughter must sit naturally on chairs BESIDE the table,
with correct proportions, posture, and physical interactions.
3. CAT: Completely recreate the unrealistic cat. Fix its deformed rear and eliminate all abnormal
protrusions. Generate a photorealistic domestic cat with anatomically correct proportions, detailed
fur, natural tail, and realistic feline movements.
4. CINEMATOGRAPHY: Make the video indistinguishable from real footage shot on professional
cinema cameras. Use natural lighting, realistic shadows, authentic skin textures, cinematic color
grading, shallow depth of field, 35mm/50mm lenses, and 24 fps motion. Include emotional
close-ups, realistic acting, smooth camera movements, and seamless transitions.
5. CONTINUITY: Follow the screenplay's exact narrative, actions, emotions, and chronology.
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, environments, and spatial relationships.
AVOID: Wooden figures, CGI appearance, plastic skin, anatomical errors, oversized furniture,
distorted animals, floating objects, clipping, morphing, or inconsistent faces.
